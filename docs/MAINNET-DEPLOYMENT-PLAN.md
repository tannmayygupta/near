## 1. The factory deposit, established (not assumed)
* **Source** (`near-daos/sputnik-dao-contract`, `sputnikdao-factory2`): `create` is `#[payable]` and has **no minimum-deposit check**; it forwards the attached deposit to the new account, and `on_create` **refunds the whole deposit** to the caller if creation fails. The protocol, not the factory, decides what is enough.
* **Live mainnet data** (read-only, nearblocks + RPC): the last 25 successful `create` calls attached **0.04 to 6 NEAR, median 0.09**. A DAO this factory created (`r6h2b.sputnik-dao.near`) has **no stored code** (it points at a *global contract*) and uses **14,729 bytes** of state (about 0.15 NEAR).
* **Conclusion:** 6 NEAR is **not** a requirement; it is a generous treasury (the e2e needs the DAO to hold at least 1.5 NEAR for a 0.6 NEAR budget). Whatever is attached beyond storage simply becomes the DAO treasury; nothing is lost.
* **Option, not applied:** attaching **2.5 NEAR** instead of 6 would cut the owner outflow from 15.15 to 11.65 NEAR (hold 12.5). It changes the plan you reviewed, so it needs your decision (one constant in `src/near/setup-plan.ts` plus the doc numbers and tests).

## 4. Security assumptions, reviewed
| Topic | What is true | Residual risk | Mitigation |

| **Vault key custody** | The vault is a full-access key generated by `setup`, stored in `<QT_SECRET_DIR>/mainnet-config.json` and loaded by the worker process. ## 5. Tests, reconciled
* **19 public methods** in `lib.rs`: 1 init + 5 DAO-only + 1 proposed-DAO + 5 agent-only + **7 distinct view methods**. The production wasm exports **20** (the SDK adds `contract_source_metadata`); **21** counting the feature-gated `migrate`, which is not in the deployed wasm. **11** are access-restricted. Verified again today (source and wasm: 0 missing, 0 unexpected).
* `mainnet:test` = the 7 views plus `contract_source_metadata`, no transactions. `mainnet:test -- --auth` = **11 signed transactions** that must fail (about 0.0126 NEAR in total on testnet).
* **What an unauthorised-call test proves:** the call was rejected inside the contract and `get_stats` is identical before and after. **What it cannot prove:** which check rejected it, because the release wasm drops panic text; only `set_paused(false)` is decisive (it would succeed if its guard were missing). `accept_dao` is refused by the "no handover pending" check, not the identity check. The identity of each guard rests on the 36 Rust tests, which assert the messages (they passed today in the pinned image).
* **The e2e, measured on the fresh rehearsal (budget 0.3 NEAR):** 25/25 checks; 27 transactions (2 DAO proposals, 4 votes, 5 agent calls, 16 vault calls); the DAO gave up exactly the 0.3 NEAR budget (6.000 -> 5.700); gas and storage registrations cost the vault 0.0238, the agent 0.0083, the three council accounts about 0.003 and the contract 0.0029 NEAR; payees received their tokens; the owner paid nothing.

## 8. Rollback and recovery limits
* Setup is idempotent: a failed step is resumed by running it again; nothing is sent twice. Created accounts cannot be un-created, but each can be deleted with its own key (its balance goes to a beneficiary you choose); the contract account can be deleted **only before the lock**.
* The swap is a real market trade and cannot be reversed; the price floor (2 USDC per NEAR) bounds the worst fill.
* If the e2e fails after the funding vote, the DAO can cancel the mandate and the worker returns the remainder.
* After the lock: no rollback of any kind for the contract.

## 9. Money at risk
* **Maximum financial exposure: about 15.45 NEAR** (everything the owner can send). It stays in accounts you control; it is lost only if the secret folder is lost or leaked.
* **Irreversibly consumed:** about 0.07 NEAR setup gas, about 0.04 NEAR e2e gas and registrations, the pool's 0.3% fee on at most 0.6 NEAR, and any slippage down to the floor (at most about US$1). The ~3.7 NEAR of contract storage is recoverable unless you lock.
